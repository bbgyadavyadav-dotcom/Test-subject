LINDISFARNE
June, 793 AD. On Lindisfarne, a quiet island monastery off England’s coast,
monks saw strange ships in the mist — long, fast, dragon-headed.
They were Vikings — warriors from Norway who had crossed
four hundred miles of open sea, guided only by the sun, the stars, and seabirds.
